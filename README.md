# FUTURE_DS_01 – Supermarket Sales Analysis & Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Internship Task

This project was completed as **Task 1** of the **Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship**.

The objective of this task was to analyze supermarket sales transaction data, clean and prepare the dataset, perform exploratory analysis, and create an interactive Power BI dashboard to identify useful sales and business insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Clean and prepare the sales transaction dataset.
* Handle inconsistent and missing data.
* Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA).
* Analyze sales and order patterns.
* Analyze time-based sales trends.
* Analyze customer-related transaction patterns.
* Analyze geographic sales performance.
* Create meaningful KPIs.
* Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
* Generate actionable business insights from the analysis.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Excel** – Data inspection and data preparation
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
* **DAX** – KPI and analytical measures

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

The raw dataset was inspected and prepared before visualization.

The major data preparation steps included:

* Checking the structure and data types of the dataset.
* Inspecting missing and inconsistent values.
* Cleaning and standardizing date fields.
* Creating a clean date field for analysis.
* Checking transaction-related values.
* Removing or handling problematic records where required.
* Preparing the cleaned dataset for Power BI.
* Creating additional analytical fields required for time-based analysis.

---

## 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis

The analysis focused on understanding:

* Overall sales performance.
* Order and transaction volume.
* Sales trends over time.
* Daily sales patterns.
* Hourly transaction patterns.
* Customer contribution to sales.
* Geographic sales distribution.
* Relationship between sales and order volume.

# 📈 Power BI Dashboard

The final dashboard was developed in Power BI and organized into four analytical pages.

## 1. Overview

The Overview page provides a high-level summary of the sales data.

### KPIs

* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Total Quantity
* Average Order Value

### Visualizations

* Monthly Sales Trend
* Sales by Day
* Sales by Hour
* Monthly Order Volume

### Filters

* Invoice Date
* Country

---

## 2. Time & Pattern

This page focuses on identifying sales and transaction patterns across different time periods.

### KPIs

* Peak Sales Hour
* Peak Sales Day

### Visualizations

* Orders by Hour
* Quantity by Hour
* Average Order Value by Day
* Day × Hour Sales Analysis

This page helps identify periods of higher and lower transaction activity.

---

## 3. Customer Analysis

This page focuses on customer-level sales and transaction behavior.

### KPIs

* Total Customers
* Average Customer Spend
* Orders per Customer

### Visualizations

* Top 10 Customers by Sales
* Top 10 Customers by Orders
* Customer Sales Contribution
* Customer Detail Analysis

---

## 4. Geographic Analysis

This page analyzes sales and customer activity across different countries available in the dataset.

### KPIs

* Countries Served
* Total Sales
* Total Orders

### Visualizations

* Top 10 Countries by Sales
* Top 10 Countries by Orders
* Top 10 Countries by Customers
* Country Sales vs Order Volume

---

## 🎛️ Dashboard Features

The Power BI dashboard includes:

* Interactive slicers

* KPI cards
* Time-based analysis
* Customer analysis
* Geographic analysis

The global filters are designed to provide a consistent filtering experience across the dashboard pages.
